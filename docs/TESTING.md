## OpenGL captures (opt-in)

Run with a display or under Xvfb. All passed on llvmpipe:

- `./tools/render_lighting.sh`: lantern shadows on/off with no glow outside the floor; an L room in both shadow modes where a glow and a paint mark placed at the notch's corner leave the notch at background luminance; 50 % versus 100 % render scale with a HUD mean luminance difference of 0.002 and zero framing offset; a wide (Last Supper) and a tall (Arnolfini) painting keeping their proportions on the wall. The same room on floors 1, 2 and 3 measures a mean luminance of about 0.05, 0.14 and 0.25, and a foe in the cellar renders about seven times brighter than the floor beside it.
- `./tools/render_ui.sh`: 20 renderer checks across title, desktop and touch HUD (now with keys, bombs, wards, the held item and Item/Bomb buttons), pause, secret entry, settings, journal and rewards; a real lantern lights the stone HUD while text brightness stays stable.
- `./tools/render_stress.sh`: the largest room with every relic, Paint Sprayer, two moths, a buff, furniture and fourteen foes. On llvmpipe at 1280×720: about 1,140 draw calls at Low effects detail and 1,800 at High (projectile trails follow the detail level), 24 MB texture memory, room transition 12–18 ms. These numbers describe the workload only; software rendering says nothing about phone frame rates.

## Packaged builds

`./tools/verify_pack.sh` exports the Android, Linux and iOS presets as resource packs, then runs each pack with `--verify-catalog`: every content family, 60 relics and 11 weapons loaded without script errors, and every imported painting must be inside the pack. Each pack then starts a run with `--quickstart` and plays 240 frames without script errors. All three presets pass.

A debug APK was exported with the official 4.6.3 Android template and Godot’s template (non-Gradle) path, as `tools/export_android.sh` does. Google’s SDK host was unreachable, so Ubuntu’s `apksigner` 31.0.2, `zipalign` and `adb` stood in for the SDK; the helper itself also wants SDK Platform 35, which a template export does not use. The result: about 57 MB, signed with APK Signature Scheme v2 and v3 by a local debug key, package `org.emberkeep.game` 0.1.0 (code 1), min SDK 24, target SDK 36, `arm64-v8a` and `x86_64` libraries, landscape, no permissions. The game files packed inside the APK passed `--verify-catalog` and played 240 `--quickstart` frames without script errors under desktop Godot.

On a Pixel 10 (PowerVR D-Series DXT-48-1536, Android 17) that APK crashed 14–15 s after every launch, in the menu or in play. Android's own crash record placed it in the audio callback thread with a read just past a large heap block. Godot mixes a forward loop up to and including `loop_end`, and the 12-second menu music set `loop_end` to its frame count, so every wrap read one frame beyond the sample data. Desktop allocators leave slack after such a block; Android's puts a guard page there. Valgrind on desktop shows the same invalid read before the fix and none after it, and the `smoke` suite now checks every looping stream. The fixed build has not yet been confirmed on the phone. No IPA was built here: iOS needs the macOS workflow, which runs `tools/test.sh` before building.

## Not yet verified

- **Painting reproductions are not bundled.** The image hosts (Wikimedia Commons, NGA, the Met, AIC) were unreachable from this environment, so every record is `pending`. Run `python3 tools/import_paintings.py` on a machine that can reach them, review the downloaded files and `docs/PAINTING_CREDITS.md`, then `--check`. Until then each painting slot shows a covered canvas and the viewer says the reproduction awaits import.
- **Water Lilies (1906)** still needs per-image verification through the AIC API; Woman with a Parasol holds its place in the collection.
- **Device performance.** Profile a representative Android phone and iPhone with `tools/render_stress.sh` (frame-time distribution, draw calls, texture residency, room-transition stalls) and tune effects budgets, the Balanced mobile preset and the 60/30 FPS target on a named device.
- **Touch ergonomics** of the Item/Bomb/Dash cluster, safe-area layout on notched screens, Android Back, suspend/resume, and the physical keyboard path for the hidden code.
- **Desktop VSync** reports the driver's effective mode; llvmpipe under Xvfb refuses VSync changes, so the On/Off switch could not be observed taking effect.
