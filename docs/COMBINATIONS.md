# Combinations

Spells, relics, held items and the floor now combine. There are three kinds of combination, 41 in all. Each one is hinted at until you find it, and the journal's new **Combos** tab keeps every one you have found. **This page lists them all, so it spoils the discovery.**

![Reactions on foes: Shatter and Freeze](images/synergy-reactions.png)

## Reactions: elements meeting on a foe

A foe carries the elements that last touched it for four seconds. A small glint in each element's colour hangs above it. When a different element lands on it, the two react: the reaction's name rises from the foe, and the element it carried is used up. A foe reacts at most about once a second (a boss about every two seconds). Elements that land in the same blow never react with each other.

Where elements come from:

- **Spells.** A spellbook carries its own element. Chili Oil adds ember, Winter Mint frost, Bitter Tea venom and Copper Wire storm.
- **The floor.** A foe standing in water is soaked (tide), in wax waxed, and on ice chilled.
- **Held items.** The Copper Kettle soaks what you hit, the Winter Globe chills every foe in the room, the Ember Flask and the Pocket Hearth set foes alight, and moths carry your spellbook's element.
- **Teammates.** In co-op, a teammate's element reacts with yours on the same foe.

| Reaction | Elements | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shatter | frost + ember | A heavy burst on the foe, which also strikes foes beside it |
| Steam | ember + tide | Scalding steam hurts and slows everything near |
| Blight | ember + venom | Fire and poison spread to every foe close by, and scorch them |
| Overload | ember + storm | The foe explodes |
| Wildfire | ember + wax | It burns long and hot and pools burning wax beneath it |
| Freeze | frost + tide | Frozen solid: no movement or attacks, and 50% more damage taken (bosses freeze only briefly) |
| Brittle | frost + venom | 40% more damage taken for five seconds |
| Set Fast | frost + wax | Held in place |
| Surge | storm + tide | Lightning strikes every soaked foe in the room, and every foe standing in water |
| Prism | glass + radiance | Six seeking beams burst from the foe |
| Flare | radiance + any other | Laid bare: 30% more damage taken for five seconds |
| Refract | glass + any other | Four shards burst out, carrying the element that struck |

## Pairings: two relics together

Two relics carried together unlock something new. When a pairing completes, it is announced, and a small sigil in its colour turns above your spellbook. Pairings that change your spells travel with the spells, so they work the same for co-op guests.

| Pairing | Relics | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Powder Keg | Chili Oil + Firecracker | Foes you set burning explode when they fall |
| Icicle | Winter Mint + Splinter | Piercing spells shatter chilled foes as they pass through |
| Ricochet Arc | Copper Wire + Skipping Stone | Every ricochet throws lightning at the nearest foe |
| Royal Jelly | Honeycomb + Bitter Tea | A foe you poisoned heals you half a heart when it falls |
| Starfall | Sunseed + Shattered Star | Every split shard bursts where it lands |
| Hunter's Moon | Moon Shard + Hunter's Mark | Weaving spells finish off any foe below a quarter of its health (not bosses) |
| Lucky Star | Lucky Penny + Red Thread | Critical hits leap to a second foe at full strength |
| Northern Lights | Storm Lantern + Deep Freeze | Lightning freezes the foes it leaps to |
| Homecoming | Return Address + Seeking Spark | Returning spells strike twice as hard on the way home |
| Crossfire | Backward Glance + Side Candles | Spells behind and beside you curve toward foes and pierce one |
| Mirror Maze | Mirror Box + Long Match | Each ricochet makes a spell 30% stronger |
| Candlemaker | Warm Wax + Wide Wick | Spells leave wax where they fly; foes walking through it are waxed |
| Constellation | Firefly Jar + Tiny Planets | Orbiting lights cast little spells at nearby foes |
| Magpie | Lodestone + Pocket Change | Each coin gives +20% damage for five seconds, three at most |
| Bramble Ward | Thorn Crown + Amber Guard | When your guard takes a blow, a ring of thorns flies out |
| Snow Angel | Snowglobe + Second Wind | Your dash nova freezes foes solid |
| Frenzy | Sugar Rush + Hungry Flame | Each kill doubles your casting speed for two seconds |
| Last Stand | Heartstone + Last Light | Below half health, spells pierce and every kill heals a quarter heart |
| Ember Dash | Cinder Steps + Chili Oil | Dashing through foes sets them burning and marks them with your book's element |
| Hearthkeeper | Eternal Hearth + Dried Rose | Every room you clear gives you a ward |
| Glass Cannon | Glass Sun + Heavy Ember | Foes that fall shatter into four glass shards |

In co-op, pairings that answer a kill (Frenzy, Last Stand, Glass Cannon) answer any foe the party fells.

## Resonance: relics that sing with your spellbook

Relics of the same element as the spellbook in your hand grow stronger with it. The more resonant relics you carry, the stronger the resonance.

| Element | Resonance | Relics | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ember | Spreading Fire | Chili Oil, Living Coal, Firecracker, Sunseed, Little Supernova, Cinder Steps | When a burning foe falls, its fire leaps to one nearby foe per resonant relic |
| Frost | Deep Cold | Winter Mint, Deep Freeze, Aurora, Snowglobe | A few quick frost hits freeze a foe solid; each resonant relic needs one fewer (at least two) |
| Storm | Thunderhead | Copper Wire, Storm Lantern, Aurora | Lightning leaps to one more foe per resonant relic |
| Venom | Virulence | Bitter Tea, Honeycomb, Blood Orange | Poison builds with every hit |
| Radiance | Dawn | Seeking Spark, Living Coal, Bottled Dawn, Dark Star | Spells lay foes bare (30% more damage) for longer per resonant relic |
| Glass | Facets | Shattered Star, Splinter, Ghost Thread, Glass Sun, Grave Glass | One more split shard per resonant relic, and shards strike harder |
| Wax | Molten | Warm Wax, Wide Wick, Heavy Ember, Steady Hand | Spells leave wax as they fly, and waxed foes are slowed |
| Tide | Undertow | Skipping Stone, Moon Shard, Mirror Box | Soaked foes take 12% more damage from everything per resonant relic |

Classic spellbooks without a written element take one from what they do: burning books are ember, chilling books frost, chaining books storm and seeking books radiance.

## Finding them

Discovery is per profile:

- **A reaction** is found the first time you see it.
- **A pairing** is found the first time you complete it.
- **A resonance** is found the first time a resonant relic meets your spellbook.

Before that, the journal shows only a riddle.

Reliquary and shop cards carry hints:

- A relic that would complete a pairing says *Something you carry answers this*, or names the pairing once you have found it.
- A relic that resonates with your spellbook says *Sings with your spellbook*.
- A spellbook on offer says how many of your relics would sing with it.

Offers themselves are not steered toward combinations; that is still luck.

## Balance

Combinations are meant to define a run. To answer them, foes on the third, fourth and fifth floors of a climb have 10%, 20% and 30% more health. Reactions scale with the blow that sets them off, with a floor-scaled minimum.

## Code

- `scripts/items/synergies.gd`: every reaction, pairing and resonance, with the lookups for them.
- `DungeonEnemy.touch()`: elements landing and reacting.
- `EmberShot.hit_enemy()` and `ricochet()`: spell pairings.
- `Adventurer`: Constellation, Bramble Ward, Snow Angel, Crossfire and Last Stand.
- `main.gd`: kill pairings, Hearthkeeper, discovery and reaction words.
- `GameActions.collect_pickup()`: Magpie.

`tests/synergy.gd` checks the catalog, every kind of reaction, the representative pairings, resonance, the floor and items, and the hints and journal. `tests/render_synergy.gd` is an opt-in capture; set `EMBERKEEP_SYNERGY_CAPTURE`.
